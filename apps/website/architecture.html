The M-Pesa On/Off Ramp Bridge

M-Pesa pushes webhooks (Daraja STK pushes) to our Middleware. The Middleware immediately communicates with our Celo smart contracts to mint or transfer equivalent cKES securely using idempotency tracking to avoid double spend via `MPESA99X` receipt tags.

Offline Batches

The mobile wallet buffers up to 20 queue items without a network connection. Once connection binds, a single EIP-712 formatted payload bursts out via the Relayer Node, decreasing base validation gas costs by up to 92% compared to sequential sending.
